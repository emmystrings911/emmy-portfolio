Research: sweet potato flour quality and safety

Food safety and nutrition

Co-evaluated the nutritional, microbial and heavy-metal profile of five white sweet potato flour samples, sun-dried and freeze-dried. The flours were nutritionally useful, with carbohydrate at 76.6–81.8% and protein at 2.4–8.3%, and free of E. coli, Salmonella, Shigella and Aspergillus. Cadmium and arsenic were above the study’s limits in every sample, so we recommended soil monitoring and source verification before promoting the flour as a wheat substitute.
