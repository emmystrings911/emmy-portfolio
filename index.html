About me

I’m a Doctor of Pharmacy graduate from the University of Jos who combines pharmacy training with software development and data analysis. I build mobile apps with React Native and Flutter, and I use Excel, Python and SQL to turn data into clear findings, from questionnaire results to assay and dissolution studies.

I founded the Pharmacy Research Hub to get more students involved in research, and I enjoy teaching, from WAEC and JAMB lessons to peer tutorials. I’m looking for work where healthcare, research and technology meet.
